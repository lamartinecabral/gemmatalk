About GemmaTalk

A private, browser-based chat powered by the Gemma 4 E2B model.

Runs 100% locally in your browser using WebAssembly and WebGPU. Your prompts and responses never leave your device.

AI may generate incorrect details, especially with small models. Verify key facts before relying on them.

Requires a WebGPU-compatible browser. The ~1.9 GB model is downloaded once and cached for offline use.
